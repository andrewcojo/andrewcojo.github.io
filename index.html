About

Hey, I'm Andrew.

I'm a mix engineer and musician. I grew up in the Chicago suburbs, and these days I work out of Central Virginia, mixing songs for artists, bands, and churches wherever they are.

Most of what I mix lives somewhere between CCM and worship, indie pop, ambient, and rock and roll. Whatever the genre, the goal is the same. I want your song to feel the way it does in your head, and I'll keep working on it until it does.
